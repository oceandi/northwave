The engine

A tree, a table, and a kit

One plain-text table and a clock. Seven verbs. Nothing locks.

TREE

The engine asks. Each branch is a request: what to record, how, and why it matters — where it plays, when, how loud. Green is delivered; red is needed. Right-click a slot and record now.

TABLE

The whole design is one readable table: page, thing, when, level, bank, words. Beds loop at 0.5; acts speak at 1.0. A person can read it; so can an engine.

KIT

A folder a game can read: your table, your recordings, and a small engine. Adapters for Wwise, FMOD, Godot, Unreal, and Unity. Ordinary CSV and WAV — your work is never locked to this app.
