Northwave Sound Engine

Game engine sound shell · Lonestar Games · macOS

Northwave turns a game's sound design into a request list. Every sound the game needs is a named slot with a reason attached — where it plays, when, how loud, and why it matters. There is no generated audio and there never will be: a person records every sound, with whatever microphone they have, in the world they are in.

  • Tree — the engine asks. Each branch is a request: what to record, how, and why.
  • Table — one plain-text table and a clock: page, thing, when, level, bank, words.
  • Kit — a folder a game can read: your table, your recordings, and a small engine. Adapters for Wwise, FMOD, Godot, Unreal, and Unity. Ordinary CSV and WAV — nothing locks.
